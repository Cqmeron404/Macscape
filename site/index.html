Where does the weather go?

Macscapes draws above your wallpaper and behind your windows. Snow settles on window edges and the Dock. Your clicks pass through to whatever you’re working on.

How do I change the weather?

Click the weather icon in the menu bar to choose an effect, adjust intensity and wind, or pause. In Rain, choose Drain the Water to clear a flood.

How do I make a snowball?

Hold Option and move the pointer along fallen snow to pack a ball. Release while moving to throw it, or stack three balls to build a snowman.

Can snow land on desktop icons?

Choose Solid Desktop Icons in the menu and allow Accessibility access in System Settings. Macscapes uses it to find the icons.

How do I quit or remove the app?

Choose Quit Macscapes from the menu bar. To uninstall it, drag Macscapes from Applications to the Trash.

Bring it to your Mac.

Free for Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Installation instructions
  1. Unzip the download and drag Macscapes into Applications.
  2. Open Macscapes. If macOS blocks the first launch, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, select Open Anyway for Macscapes, and confirm.
  3. Click the weather icon in the menu bar to pick your first effect.

Free and open source. You can inspect the code before installing Macscapes.
